## English

These skills are **informational and educational tools**. They do not constitute legal, tax, accounting, or any professional advice, and they create no attorney-client or accountant-client relationship.

- **Not advice.** The content summarizes general laws, regulations, and practices. Your specific situation may call for a different analysis.

Confirm any figure against primary official sources (DOF, SAT/IMPI/PROFECO portals, statute texts from the Cámara de Diputados).
